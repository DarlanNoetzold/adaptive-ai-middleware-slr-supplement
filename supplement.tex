\section*{Scope}
This document accompanies the article \emph{A Systematic Review of Adaptive Artificial Intelligence Middleware for Quantum-Ready Smart Environments} (submitted to ACM Computing Surveys) and provides the three study-level tables that the article references as Supplementary Material. Study identifiers (A01--A74) follow Appendix~A of the main article; the bibliographic references cited in the tables are listed at the end of this document. Table~\ref{tab:scope_compliance} reports the scope-compliance reassessment of the 74 included studies (referenced in Section~3.2 of the main article), Table~\ref{tab:qa_scores} reports the quality-appraisal scores under the nine-criterion rubric (Section~3.4), and Table~\ref{tab:taxonomy_index} indexes the study identifiers behind each category of the four taxonomy figures (Section~4.3).
